# GetShap Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 13 August 2026

This privacy policy applies to the GetShap application.

GetShap is built to respect your privacy. The app does not collect, share or
sell any personal information about you.

## Data collection

The app does not request, store on our side, or transmit:

- personal details (name, e-mail address, phone number),
- location data,
- device identifiers,
- usage analytics,
- crash logs,
- files, photos or contact information,
- or any other form of personal data.

Everything you enter – your questionnaire answers, training level, streak and
settings – is stored **on your own device only**, in the app's own storage. The
developer has no server and no database, and cannot access it. Deleting the app
deletes this data.

If system-level backup is enabled on your device (iCloud or Google backup), your
operating system may include the app's data in its backups, under Apple's or
Google's rules and your device settings.

## Internet connection

The app works offline. It connects to the network for one purpose only: to check
whether a newer version is available.

- **iOS:** it queries Apple's public app lookup service (`itunes.apple.com`)
  using the app's identifier. As with any internet request, Apple sees your
  device's IP address.
- **Android:** the check goes through Google Play services.

No personal data is sent in these requests, and the developer receives and
stores nothing from them. Apple and Google act as independent controllers here:
Apple <https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/> ·
Google <https://policies.google.com/privacy>

## Notifications

Reminders are local notifications created and shown by your own device. They do
not come from a server. You can refuse or withdraw notification permission at
any time in your device settings; the app remains usable either way.

## Third-party services

The app integrates no analytics tools, no advertising networks, and no
data-collecting SDKs.

Downloads, installs and crash statistics are handled by the App Store and Google
Play under their own privacy policies. In their developer consoles the developer
sees only aggregated, non-identifiable figures.

## Children's privacy

The app is intended for people aged 13 and over. No information is collected
from users of any age.

## Your rights

Because no data about you is collected or stored by the developer, there is
nothing held about you to access, correct or erase. The data on your device is
under your control: you can change it by retaking the questionnaire, and remove
it by uninstalling the app. If you have any question you can write to the
address below.

If you believe your data protection rights have been infringed, you may lodge a
complaint with the Hungarian National Authority for Data Protection and Freedom
of Information (NAIH), <https://naih.hu>.

## Changes to this policy

Any update to this policy will be reflected in the date at the top of this
