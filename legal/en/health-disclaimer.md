## 1. What GetShap is – and what it is not

The purpose of GetShap is to help you **come to enjoy regular exercise and
mindful eating**, make them a habit, and through that move towards your own
physical goals. The app offers general exercise and lifestyle content intended
for a broad audience.

**GetShap expressly does not aim** to provide a sport-scientifically optimised
training plan, or one built for competitive sport or peak performance.

**How the app adapts to you.** Based on your answers to the onboarding
questionnaire, it sets the starting difficulty of your workouts and the pace at
which the exercises get harder. This is an **automatic classification based
solely on your own self-reported answers** – not a professional assessment, not
a medical examination, and it does not take into account your health status,
past injuries, musculoskeletal limitations, or anything else the questionnaire
does not ask about. How well the classification fits you depends on how
accurately you answered, and even then a given difficulty level may still not be
right for you.

The nutrition suggestions do not adapt to you at all: they are general
recommendations, not a meal plan and not a diet.

In addition:

- The content was **not prepared by a healthcare professional**.
- GetShap is **not a healthcare service**, not a medical device or medical
  product, and not a rehabilitation or physiotherapy programme.
- GetShap **does not replace** examination, advice or supervision by a doctor,
  physiotherapist, dietitian or personal trainer.
- Using the app **does not create a trainer–client, doctor–patient or any other
  healthcare provider relationship** between you and the developer of GetShap.
- Nothing in the app constitutes **medical, healthcare, dietary or therapeutic
  advice**.

## 2. You exercise at your own risk

You perform the exercises, workouts and suggestions in GetShap **solely at your
own responsibility and at your own risk**. You decide whether a given exercise,
load or repetition count is appropriate for you, and you are responsible for
staying within your own limits.

Physical activity – including the workouts suggested in GetShap – **carries
inherent risks by its very nature**. These include, among others, muscle
soreness, strains, sprains, muscle, tendon and joint injuries, falls, feeling
unwell, fainting, and in rare cases serious or even life-threatening harm to
health. By using GetShap you **acknowledge and accept** this risk.

## 3. Consult a doctor first

**Talk to your doctor before you start** if any of the following applies to you:

- you have a heart or cardiovascular condition, or you have experienced chest
  pain;
- you have high blood pressure, or take medication for blood pressure or your
  heart;
- you have experienced dizziness, fainting or balance problems;
- you have a joint, bone, spine or muscle problem, or an old injury that
  exercise could aggravate;
- you have had surgery, or you are recovering from an injury;
- you are pregnant, have recently given birth, or are planning a pregnancy;
- you have a chronic condition (e.g. diabetes, asthma, epilepsy, osteoporosis);
- you take any medication regularly;
- you are over 65 and have not exercised regularly until now;
- you are unsure for any reason whether exercise is safe for you.

**If your doctor has advised against physical activity, do not use the app**
without their approval.

## 4. When to stop immediately

**Stop exercising immediately and seek medical help** if you experience any of
the following:

- chest pain, pressure in the chest, a racing or irregular heartbeat;
- dizziness, feeling faint, blurred vision;
- unusual shortness of breath that does not pass with rest;
- nausea, sudden weakness;
- sharp, stabbing or sudden joint or muscle pain;
- any symptom that feels frightening or unusual.

**Never "train through" pain.** No part of GetShap encourages you to continue
moving through pain or while feeling unwell.

## 5. Safe training is your responsibility

- Warm up before training and follow the described execution of each exercise.
- Choose only a load that you can control safely.
- Make sure your environment is safe: stable, non-slip surface, enough free
  space, safe distance from furniture and objects.
- If you do not understand an exercise or are unsure about correct form,
  **skip it** or ask a qualified professional.
- Make sure you are properly hydrated and rested.

**You** are responsible for the safety of your training space, the condition of
any equipment you use, and the correct execution of the exercises.

## 6. Nutrition content

Nutrition tips in the app are **general lifestyle information** – not dietetic
advice, not a meal plan and not a treatment. They do not take into account your
health status, allergies, food intolerances, medication or individual
nutritional needs.

Do not follow them, and **seek advice from a doctor or dietitian**, if you have
diabetes, kidney or liver disease, a metabolic disorder or a food allergy, if
you are pregnant or breastfeeding, or if you have or have had an eating
disorder.

GetShap is not designed for calorie restriction, rapid weight loss, or pushing
weight-based goals. If you are struggling with eating or with your body image,
please talk to a professional.

## 7. No guaranteed results

Changes in physical performance and body composition depend on many factors
(genetics, starting point, sleep, nutrition, stress, consistency, health
status). GetShap **does not promise or guarantee any specific result** –
whether related to fitness, weight, appearance or health.

## 8. Age and minors

GetShap is intended for people aged **13 and over**.

**Users under 18** may use the app only with the knowledge and approval of a
parent or legal guardian; in that case the legal guardian accepts this
disclaimer and is responsible for ensuring that the child understands and
follows it.

For minors we strongly recommend that workouts take place under adult
supervision, and that you consult the child's doctor before the first workout. A
growing body tolerates load differently from an adult one; for minors it is
especially important to stay within a pain-free range and to prioritise correct
form over volume.
